Our Story

Nook Notebook started in a small workshop in Portland, Oregon, with one idea: make a notebook that writers and creatives would actually keep using. Not a disposable doodle pad — a tool for the long haul.

We source our paper responsibly, assemble every notebook by hand, and pack each one with a bit of that Portland care that you can feel when you open it.
