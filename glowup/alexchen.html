About Alex

I'm a visual designer with 8 years of experience helping startups and small businesses develop brands that feel intentional and look unmistakable.

My approach is collaborative — I work closely with founders and teams to understand what makes their company unique, then translate that into a visual language that holds up across every touchpoint.

Before going independent, I led design at a Portland-based startup where we grew from 5 to 40 people. That experience taught me how to design for real growth, not just pretty pictures.
