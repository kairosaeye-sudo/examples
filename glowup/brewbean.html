Our Story

Brew & Bean opened its doors in 2019 with a simple belief: great coffee should be a daily ritual, not a luxury. We source our beans directly from small farms in Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala — roasting every batch in-house to bring out the flavors our neighbors love.

Every drink is made by hand. Every pastry is baked from scratch. And every cup comes with a smile.
