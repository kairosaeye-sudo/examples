Recommended test sequence

Round 1: Run the current (offer-led) variant against the problem-led variant on each ad. Problem-led usually wins on cold traffic — higher emotional stakes = scroll-stop.

Round 2: Once you have a winner, test the social proof variant against it. Social proof wins on warm retargeting — people who already know you want validation, not a pitch.

Rotate: Swap in a fresh variant every 2–3 weeks to fight creative fatigue. The research says most winning ads saturate around 4–6 weeks.

A/B test rules (from the research)

  • Give each variant at least 5 days and $10–15/day before drawing conclusions — early optimization on thin data is the #1 cause of wasted test budget
  • Define your success metric before you launch (CPA? CTR? Video view-through?) and don't switch mid-test
  • Scale the winner before testing the next concept — a proven ad at $50/day is more valuable than finding the next winner untested
  • Change one element per round — same body, three openings. Hook variance beats production value
  • Keep a swipe file organized by objective, not industry — a great lead ad for a dentist often works for a law firm
